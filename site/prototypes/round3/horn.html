How to read the glass

Its wall is the curve.

  • The level is the tokens left.The right-hand scale counts down toward the mouth. The mouth widens forever, so nobody can buy the last token. Far past graduation the drawing steps back to fit, and a mark by the mouth says by how much.
  • The liquid is all the SOL.Everything below the surface is the SOL the price counts. The left-hand scale reads the real SOL, the part above the 1B ring.
  • The surface's area is the price.Its width is all the SOL; its area is SOL per token. Each step up is wider, so the level climbs slower and each token costs more.
  • The stem holds the 30 SOL that isn't there.Below the 1B ring it narrows forever and holds exactly 30 SOL. The price counts them; the vault never had them.
  • At 82.25 real SOL it graduates, inside the trade.The stem's 30 drops out and the glass tightens by 26.7% along its whole length. That lifts the level 71.43M tokens up the scale, and those tokens burn. The surface keeps its width, so the price holds. Same pool from then on, as plain x·y = k. The 1% fee never enters the glass: half to the coin's creator, half to the platform.
