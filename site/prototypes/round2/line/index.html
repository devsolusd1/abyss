2Line rules

Four things no one can change, the team included.

  1. No LP token.

    Liquidity goes into the pool once and stays there for good.

  2. No way out for pool money.

    No instruction lets anyone take a pool's SOL or tokens.

  3. One address, all its life.

    Graduation happens inside a buy. No migration, no new pool.

  4. A fixed coin.

    1,000,000,000 supply. No mint authority, no freeze authority, fixed metadata.

3The fare

1% of every trade, paid in SOL. Half goes to the coin's creator, half to the platform.
