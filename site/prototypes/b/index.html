The math

  • OpeningThe curve counts 30 virtual SOL plus the real SOL in: x = 30 + R. A coin opens at 30 SOL of market cap.
  • Buying1% comes off in SOL. With the rest, d, you get T − 30 × 10⁹ ÷ (x + d) tokens, where T is the tokens left in the pool.
  • GraduationWhen R reaches √(420 × 30) − 30 ≈ 82.25 SOL, a market cap of 420 SOL. It happens inside the buy that gets there.
  • At graduationThe 30 virtual SOL is dropped and T × 30 ÷ x tokens are burned, about 7.1% of the supply, so the price stays where it was.
  • AfterThe same pool, at the same address, carries on as plain x · y = k on the real SOL and the tokens left.
  • Fees1% of every trade, in SOL. Half to the coin's creator, half to the treasury. Nobody else takes a cut.
